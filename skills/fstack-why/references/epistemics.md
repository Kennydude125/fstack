# Epistemics

Preserve the five evidence tiers from pstack while applying them to financial observations and explanations. Every material claim belongs to a tier, and its phrasing must match that tier. Data and source statements do not automatically establish causality.

## 1. Direct

An actual source explicitly states the relevant fact, forecast or reason. A dated filing may report a value, management may explain why a margin changed, or an original decision record may state why an investor chose a model. Cite the exact source and preserve whose statement it is. Management saying a change caused growth is direct evidence of management's explanation, not independent proof of causation. A forecast is still a forecast.

## 2. Supported

Several relevant pieces of independent evidence converge on an explanation, although none directly establishes it alone. Cite each piece and the inferential link. Multiple copies of the same announcement are one source, not independent convergence. State that the evidence supports the explanation, and retain contrary evidence.

## 3. Inferred

A reasonable interpretation of context that is not explicitly established. Show the observations and reasoning that lead to it, and phrase it as an interpretation. A company improving cash flow while inventory falls may support an inference about working capital; it does not establish every claimed business cause or future result.

## 4. Speculative

A plausible hypothesis on thin evidence, with other explanations fitting as well. Label it as a possibility and explain the observation that could distinguish it. A generic industry narrative or imagined catalyst cannot be presented as a verified market expectation.

## 5. Unknown

The relevant source or explanation could not be established. Record what was searched, the dates and query scope, what was found, and the missing evidence. Unavailable consensus, private transactions or missing publication dates are useful research limitations. Unknown is a valid result.

## Avoid rationalization and sycophancy

Do not work backward from the current price or later earnings outcome to invent a clean historical explanation. A user-provided thesis is one hypothesis to test. Being persuasive or unusual is not evidence. Do not mistake absence of a mention for evidence that a risk did not exist. Keep model knowledge of later outcomes as an explicit hindsight limitation.

## Contradictory sources

Show both material sources and their dates, periods, units and accounting conventions. First check whether the contradiction is a timing or definition mismatch. Do not average incompatible figures or select the source that supports the preferred thesis. Preserve any unresolved disagreement.

## Before finalizing

Spot-check every material citation against the actual source. Claims without adequate evidence move to the appropriate inferential tier or Unknown. Distinguish observed facts, source statements, assumptions and forecasts. Include what remains unknown and how it affects the research decision. Confidence labels are not calibrated event probabilities or a numerical trading win rate.
