# Financial source playbook

For the category assigned by fstack-why, record the actual service/document/dataset, query, date range, publication cutoff and result location. Prefer original issuer/regulatory material, identified market data, dated expectation estimates, official economic releases, original industry material, user-authorized research records and actual model history as appropriate.

Search related terms and verify the strongest original evidence. Trace syndicated reports to origin and distinguish latest revised data from point-in-time releases. Management explanation is a source statement. Missing analyst consensus stays unavailable; a valuation-derived assumption is model dependent. For every relevant category return searched, empty, unavailable or not applicable with a reason. Do not explore unrelated connected corporate chat or documents merely because a tool exists.
