# Investigation

1. Route through how; reasons also route through why. Establish a dated common evidence packet and relevant source coverage.
2. Record throughput checkpoint as not applicable to a read-only investigation. Keep external records behaviorally read-only.
3. Produce the how-shaped explanation or an evidence-backed comparison. Company and ETF work checks business/fund mechanics, expectations and available valuation evidence. Portfolio work uses supplied positions and states missing constraints.
4. Apply unslop and preserve citations, confidence tiers and gaps. No PR is required. If a change is requested, reroute to the appropriate construction or correction playbook.
