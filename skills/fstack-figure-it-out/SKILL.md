# Figure it out

Design one auditable workflow when no narrower playbook fits. Read the main mode's principles, then open Frame, Design, Run, Record and Verify.

## A. Frame

Ground the question before committing. Define a falsifiable completion predicate for the research artifact, quantify work and blockers, and choose rigor based on stakes. Present the framing before a long run. A completion predicate can be a completed evaluation protocol, never guaranteed alpha or a target historical return.

## B. Design

Decompose into independently verifiable units, riskiest unknown first. Establish baseline inputs and verification before new calculations. Use architect/arena for consequential structural choices, not mechanical settled work. Parallelize only across clear ownership seams. Write the phase list and gates the user can review.

## C. Run

Each unit states its hypothesis, makes the smallest scoped change, measures against the actual artifact and retains or rejects it with a reason. Verify units in dependency order. Pair delegated work with an independent verifier. Use VERIFIED, NOT VERIFIED or INCONCLUSIVE for specified checks. Inconclusive is not a pass.

## D. Record

Use [show me your work](../fstack-show-me-your-work/SKILL.md) throughout, including rejected experiments and revisions. Keep strategy search within development data; preserve evaluation isolation and all attempts.

## E. Verify

Recheck the whole against the initial predicate. Test that checks can fail on known wrong variants. Encode recurring corrections in a structural check. Deliver the workflow, rigor, actual verification, audit location and remaining gaps. Do not hide a negative or relax the finish condition to declare done.
