# Design red flags

Reject or explain designs that hide instrument/currency/time assumptions, confuse observation and publication dates, permit multiple writers to one journal, mix source statements with facts, collapse artifact checks into thesis confidence, expose evaluation data during candidate tuning, require callers to synchronize parallel booleans, or rely on unavailable providers/tools. Prefer one canonical contract with inspectable transformation lineage. Avoid a shared trading platform when a case-specific reproducible program answers the requested research question.
