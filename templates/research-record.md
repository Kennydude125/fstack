# Research record template

Use unknown rather than inventing missing data. Store filled records in the private research workspace.

## Context

Record ID and creation time/timezone; instrument, venue/security class, currency, horizon, information cutoff, price and quote time, benchmark, original observation and source.

## Falsifiable thesis

Write the predicted observable outcome, deadline, economic transmission, invalidating observation and what expectations evidence actually exists. Separate management guidance, analyst consensus and price-implied assumptions.

## Evidence

For each material claim record original source and location, period and publication date, fact/source statement/inference/assumption/forecast, Direct/Supported/Inferred/Speculative/Unknown tier, contradictions and relevance. Record negative searches and unavailable sources.

## Model and risk

Method, formulas, dated inputs, units, scenario assumptions and sensitivity. Distinguish current discounted value from future price. State costs, corporate-action treatment, currency, liquidity and relevant portfolio exposure. Do not invent personal risk capacity.

## Challenge

Strongest technical defect or economic objection, alternate explanation, evidence and next discriminating observation. Keep unproven concerns labeled.

## Decision and verification

Separate artifact-integrity outcome, thesis support and performance evidence. Record actual user decision or no decision. Link the exact manifest/receipt and next review event. Append later corrections and outcomes without changing original predictions.
